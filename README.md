# Scout for Mac

Scout is a Mac app that gives your AI agents a Google Chrome window of their own, so they can look things up and use websites for you. Its window shows you what they are doing and tells you when they need you.

## Install

1. Download **Scout-0.17.1.dmg** from the [latest release](../../releases/latest).
2. Open it and drag **Scout** to **Applications**.
3. Open Scout and click **Connect Scout to my agents**.

## What you need

- A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) running macOS 13.5 or later.
- Google Chrome.

## Updates

Scout updates itself. To check now, choose **Scout ▸ Check for Updates…** from the menu bar.
